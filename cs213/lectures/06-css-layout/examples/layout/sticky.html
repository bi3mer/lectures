Here is some text about the purpose of my made-up blog.

  • Recreo - Dynamite
  • Nero - at least it looks nice.
  • Starbucks - it's fine.
  • Blue Bottle - worth the line.
  • Peet's - strong.
  • Dunkin - it was open.
  • Tim Hortons - fine.
  • La Colombe - the draft latte.
  • Intelligentsia - bright.
  • Stumptown - cold brew.
  • Joe - a good cortado.
  • Gregorys - loud.
  • Blank Street - quick.
  • Birch - quiet upstairs.
  • Think - good for work.
  • Ninth Street - espresso.
  • Devoción - the beans.
  • Sey - light roasts.

Every review is one shop, one drink, and one rating out of five. The list grows each time I find a new place, and the ratings change each time I go back. Some shops are close to campus and some are a train ride away. I go back to the good ones more than I should.
